Smart computer — Update 7.9.4.43

1) Sales shipping expense
- Added shipping amount to the payment/invoice screen.
- Shipping can be charged to the customer or deducted from invoice profit.
- Default mode is "customer" and can be changed from Settings.
- Customer-paid shipping is included in invoice grand total and shown on customer receipt/export.
- Profit-paid shipping does not increase the customer total and is deducted in profit reporting.

2) Purchase paid amount
- New purchase invoices automatically set Paid = Invoice Total while the total is being built.
- The value remains editable; once manually changed it is not overwritten automatically.

3) Invoice editing
- Sales invoices can be edited with stock, customer, account, shift and FIFO effects recalculated.
- Purchase invoices can be edited with stock delta, FIFO remaining batches, supplier balance and accounts recalculated.
- For purchase safety, the original warehouse is fixed during editing and quantities cannot be reduced below amounts already consumed/sold from that purchase.
- Sales invoices linked to a sales return are protected from editing until the return is cancelled.

4) PWA
- New service-worker cache build to prevent stale code after update.
