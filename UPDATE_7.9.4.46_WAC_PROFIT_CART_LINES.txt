Smart computer v7.9.4.47 — Weighted Average Profit + Cashier Cart Lines

1) Sales profit/cost now uses the current weighted-average product cost (WAC) captured at sale time instead of old FIFO batch cost.
2) FIFO/FEFO batches can still be consumed for stock batch tracking, while profit uses WAC consistently.
3) Sale invoice items store weightedAverageCostTotal and costPriceAtSale from WAC; returns reuse the original sale cost snapshot.
4) Financial reports prefer the WAC snapshot and the FIFO label was corrected to Weighted Average Cost.
5) Cashier cart now shows live invoice profit after invoice discount, based on the current average cost.
6) Cart lines have unique IDs. If a line price is manually changed, clicking the product again adds/uses a separate original-price line instead of changing the edited line.
7) +/- quantity, price editing, unit changing, and deleting target the exact cart line, so duplicated product lines can coexist safely.
8) Cache/version bumped so the new logic loads immediately after deployment.
